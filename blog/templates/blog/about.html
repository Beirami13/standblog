about us

more about us!

Stand Blog is a place to write and share ideas. Anyone can register for a free account, publish posts under different categories, and take part in the conversation by liking and commenting on the posts they enjoy. Whether you're here to read or to write, we hope you find something worth your time.

The platform is built with Django and kept deliberately simple: no ads, no clutter — just posts, categories, and a community of readers and writers.

Our Mission

We want writing and sharing a blog post to be as simple as possible — no unnecessary steps, no distractions. Just create an account, write, and publish.

Who It's For

Stand Blog is open to anyone who wants to write — whether that's a personal journal, a technical tutorial, or your thoughts on something you care about. Browse by category to find what interests you.

Write & Publish

Create an account and publish your own posts under the category that fits them best.

Like & Comment

Engage with posts you enjoy — leave a comment, reply to others, or simply like the ones that stand out to you.

Browse by Category

Not sure where to start? Use the categories sidebar to explore posts on the topics that interest you most.
